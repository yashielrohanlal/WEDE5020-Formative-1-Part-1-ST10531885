Our History

Founded in 2022, RS Labs started as a small sports-science research initiative aimed at solving thermal overload and friction injuries in powerlifters and endurance athletes.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To empower athletes globally by creating science-backed activewear that enhances physical potential.

Vision: To lead the athletic apparel industry through continuous textile innovation and sustainable manufacturing.
