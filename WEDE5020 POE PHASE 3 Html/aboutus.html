Our History

Founded in 2022, RS Labs started as a small sports-science research initiative aimed at solving thermal overload and friction injuries in powerlifters and endurance athletes.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To empower athletes globally by creating science-backed activewear that enhances physical potential and protects against fatigue.

Vision: To lead the athletic apparel industry through continuous textile innovation, biomechanical engineering, and sustainable manufacturing.

Textile & Fabric Innovations FAQ

Our proprietary thermal compression blend combines moisture-wicking synthetic fibers with high-tensile elastane to actively pull heat away from active muscle zones while retaining joint warmth.

Friction-Lock weaves utilize seamless ultrasonic stitching and low-friction outer coatings, reducing inner-thigh chafing and skin tearing by up to 85% under heavy load.
